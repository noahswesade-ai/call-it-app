Underdog — Question Bank

Every question in the game, by category. Edit freely: add, delete or change lines, then tell Claude "sync the question bank" and the game is updated.

One question per line: [Type · Difficulty] Question => Answer = number

  • Answer: one right answer (Easy/Medium: first to shout it; Hard: 3-2-1 countdown, both answer at once).
  • Number: closest number wins. Put the real number at the end after " = ", e.g. "=> 8,849 m = 8849".
  • Turns: take turns naming things. No answer needed.
  • Challenge: a task or dare; the host picks the winner. No answer needed.
  • Friend / Friend number: about {friend}, who secretly types the real answer. No difficulty.
  • Room game: Know Your Friends games run by the room (Truth or Dare, Hot Seat…). These may repeat.

Difficulty is Easy, Medium or Hard. Keep the category headings as they are.
